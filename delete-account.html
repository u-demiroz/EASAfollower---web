Account Deletion Request

As per App Store and Google Play guidelines, you can request the complete deletion of your account and associated data.

What happens when I delete my account?

  • Your profile and login credentials will be permanently erased.
  • Your chat history and saved documents will be completely deleted from our servers.
  • Any active subscriptions must be cancelled through your Apple ID or Google Play account separately.

Alternatively, you can instantly delete your account from within the EASA Follower app by navigating to Settings -> Account -> Delete Account.
